# Payments — assumptions awaiting TL confirmation

The project PDF lists these Payment Tracking items: candidate name, course or internship name, payment amount, payment date, payment status (Pending / Partially Paid / Paid), and upload of a payment receipt or screenshot. Admin: "track payment commitments and completed payments" and "view uploaded payment screenshots". Monthly report: "total payment commitments", "total successful payments", "total revenue generated by each BDE".
It does **not** define commitments versus receipts, how partial payments work, what counts as revenue, who verifies proof, currency, or file rules. Everything below is a technical proposal, not a company policy.

| # | Topic | What the code does now | Question for the TL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Meaning of "candidate confirmed payment"** (Interview Tracking) | Derived: true when a payment **record (commitment)** exists for the lead | Does it mean "committed to pay" (as built) or "has actually paid something"? One expression changes it (`payment-confirmed-sql.js`) |
| 2 | **Old manual flags** | Interviews whose Phase 5 flag was Yes but have no payment record now show No. Nothing is deleted. The list query is in `docs/payments-and-uploads.md` | Should BDEs re-enter those as payments, or is the old flag to be ignored? |
| 3 | Commitment vs money received | Two levels: a payment record = commitment (amount, course, **commitment date**); instalments = money received (amount, **payment date**) | Is a commitment a separate step in the company's process, or is only money received tracked? |
| 4 | Commitment date | A required date on the commitment, used to place commitments in reports (not in the PDF) | Keep it, or use the creation date? |
| 5 | Status | Derived from amounts, never typed: Pending / Partially Paid / Paid (Paid = received equals committed) | Any other status, e.g. Refunded, Cancelled, Overdue (needs a due date)? |
| 6 | Several payment records per lead | Allowed (for example two courses). Each has its own instalments | One per lead enough? |
| 7 | Over-payment | Rejected (total received cannot exceed the commitment) | Are over-payments, extra fees or refunds ever recorded? |
| 8 | Changing the commitment | A BDE can change course, amount and date at any time, but not below what was received | Discounts after the fact? Who may approve? |
| 9 | Editing and deleting | A BDE can edit own payments and instalments at any time. There is **no delete, no refund and no edit history** | Is an audit trail required? Can BDEs delete a mistaken instalment, or only Admin? |
| 10 | Dates | Future payment dates, payment dates before the commitment date, and future commitment dates are all accepted | Block any of these? |
| 11 | Currency | One currency, no symbol, amounts up to 9,999,999,999.99 | Confirm the currency and the maximum |
| 12 | **Revenue** | Sum of instalments as entered by BDEs. Nothing verifies them: revenue counts **before** any review | Should only Admin-verified instalments count as revenue? |
| 13 | "Completed payments" | Count of payments with status Paid, and the sum of their committed amounts ("Fully paid amount") | Or the money received in the period? Both are shown |
| 14 | Admin overview dates | Commitments are placed by **commitment date**; money received by **payment date**. So "received in June" can include instalments on May commitments, and outstanding is committed minus received so far against those commitments | Confirm the monthly-report semantics |
| 15 | Admin and proof | Admin can view every payment and every proof file. **No approve/reject step**, no comments | Is proof verification needed (next phase)? |
| 16 | Is proof mandatory? | No. An instalment can exist without a file. Admin can filter instalments without proof | Must every instalment have proof? |
| 17 | Receipt rules | JPEG, PNG, PDF only; 5 MB each (setting `RECEIPT_MAX_BYTES`); 5 files per instalment; no delete or replace; phone formats such as HEIC are refused | Right limits and types? |
| 18 | Duplicate warnings | Same amount and date on the same payment, and byte-identical files (any BDE), are warned about and can be confirmed. Never merged or blocked | Block across BDEs instead? Who owns a receipt two BDEs both uploaded? |
| 19 | Privacy and retention | Receipts may hold personal and bank data. Admin sees all. Files are kept forever | Retention period, who may see proof, deletion on request |
| 20 | Admin rights | Read-only for payments and proof | Should Admin correct amounts or mark payments? (needs an audit trail) |

## Not part of this phase
Proof verification workflow, refunds, deleting payments, weekly/monthly reports and Excel export, dashboard metrics beyond the payment overview.
